Stripe invoicing
Send real invoices customers can pay online with a card. Uses your own Stripe account — the money goes straight to you, nothing routes through us.
This one's set up slightly differently than Email, since a Stripe key can never be safely stored in the app itself:
  1. Get a secret key free at dashboard.stripe.com/apikeys
  2. Add it as an environment variable named STRIPE_SECRET_KEY in this site's Netlify settings (Site configuration → Environment variables)
  3. Redeploy the site — environment variable changes need a fresh deploy
Once connected, a "Send Stripe invoice" button appears on any unpaid payment. It emails the customer a real Stripe invoice they can pay online — you'll still mark it Paid in Flow yourself once you see it's settled.
